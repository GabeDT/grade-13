How this works

A projection,
not a promise.

This tool assumes your starting balance earns the same estimated annual return each month, and that each monthly contribution is added at the end of the month. It does not account for investment fees, taxes, inflation, withdrawals or changing market returns.

Real investments move up and down. A long-term return estimate is a useful way to compare scenarios, not a prediction of what you will earn.
